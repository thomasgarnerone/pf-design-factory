PatientFileMenu

Primary navigation for patient file sections. Built on SectionMenu: a horizontal tab bar (collapses to dropdown when narrow) plus a panel below. Configurable per market — and when a Health Observation session is active, a side panel (≥1280 px) or live tab (<1280 px) appears.

Configuration-driven. Tab set comes from usePatientFileConfiguration('tabs'). EHR markets see Health · Financial · Administrative · History. BMS-DE sees Administrative · History · Documents · Notes. BMS-FR/IT sees Administrative · History. Routes mount under /patients/:patientId/:tabId — note that the History tab uses timeline as its route path.
