Accordion

Progressive disclosure for long content — FAQ, form sections, help. Click the row to expand; the chevron rotates 180°. The chevron sits on the trailing edge by default; opt into a leading-edge chevron via ox-accordion__item--chevron-start for navigation-style lists where the disclosure cue should lead the title.

Default — chevron at the end

Open your appointments list from the dashboard, pick the booking, and tap Cancel. Cancellations made more than 24 hours before the slot are free.
You'll receive an SMS and an email with the new proposed slot. You can accept it in one tap, propose a different time, or cancel without fees.
Reimbursement depends on your country and plan. In France, sessions with conventionné practitioners are partially covered by Assurance Maladie; your mutuelle handles the rest.
All records are hosted on HDS-certified infrastructure in the EU. Only practitioners you book with have access to the medical information you choose to share.
