Specs

Placement'right' (default) · 'left' · 'top' · 'bottom'
Backdropbackdrop?: boolean = true — when false, no scrim is rendered and the page behind stays interactive (inspector / persistent banner pattern)
ModalityWith backdrop → role="dialog" aria-modal="true" · Without → role="complementary"
SizeSide drawers: s 32 rem · m 40 rem · l 56 rem · Top/Bottom: auto by content, max 60% of viewport
MotionSlides in from its anchored edge · 240 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1) · scrim fades 200 ms
Scrimrgba(25, 38, 52, .35) · click to dismiss (when modal)
