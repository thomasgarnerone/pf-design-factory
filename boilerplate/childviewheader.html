ChildViewHeader

Page header for "child view" routes — the expanded state of a CardCollection. Always shows a nav-back button; supports primary action and up to two icon actions inline (overflow goes into a More menu). Transparent: no card chrome.

Pairs with CardCollection. When a card has childView.to, clicking the title navigates to a route whose top is a ChildViewHeader. Visually transparent — sits directly on the page background.
