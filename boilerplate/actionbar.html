ActionBar

Sidebar action rail. Hosts a vertical column of navigation actions inside a parent layout — typically the left rail of a workspace shell.

Tiny by design. The component itself is just an <aside> with a VStack gap="xsmall"; the children determine the look. Sized 250–360 px (16vw), full-height, scrolls overflow.
