Contextual popover

An inline help/info icon that opens a bubble with contextual guidance. Sits next to the thing it explains — never free-floating.

From Oxygen core. overlays/ContextualPopover/ContextualPopover.tsx — wraps Popover/PopoverContent on desktop; falls back to a small Modal on mobile.

Billing runs on the 1st of each month for the previous month's appointments. Invoices are available in your Documents tab.
