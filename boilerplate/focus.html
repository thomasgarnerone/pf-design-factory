Focus ring

Sourced from packages/core/src/style/focus.scss. Every interactive Oxygen component (Button, IconButton, Input, Pill, Link, etc.) uses this exact recipe.

focus.scss → @mixin focus-ring inner ring: 2px solid white
outer ring: 2px solid blue-500
offset: 2px
Same ring on every interactive surface — inputs, toggles, pills, links. The white→blue double ring maintains contrast against dark, light, and branded backgrounds.
Focused link on dark
On dark surfaces, the inner white ring separates the element from the blue halo — that's why the recipe is a double ring, not a single blue outline.
