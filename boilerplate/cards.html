Cards

Three surface styles from dataDisplay/Card/v2 — CardElevated, CardOutlined, CardSolid. Each accepts a display prop that pairs a radius token with a padding scale.

Displays
Five sizes — xsmall · small · medium · large · xlarge. Each step increases both padding (S → XXXL) and corner radius (S → XL). Shown on CardOutlined so both the radius and the internal breathing room are visible.
