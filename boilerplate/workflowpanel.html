WorkflowPanel

A temporary focus panel that sits beside main content. Header has title + optional step-back, actions overflow + comfort-mode + close. Footer has cancel / optional alternative / confirm. Switches to drawer at md/lg breakpoints; protects unsaved changes via a CloseDialog.

Layout contract. Sticky to the right at lg (≥1024px) using --workspace-size; in comfort mode it expands to ½ workspace; below md it becomes a bottom/right drawer.
