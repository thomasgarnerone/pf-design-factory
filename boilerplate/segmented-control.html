<SegmentedControl /> · recreation of packages/core/src/navigation/SegmentedControl/SegmentedControl.scss & _styles.module.scss. A single-select pill container used for mutually-exclusive views — e.g. Day / Week / Month toggles or an agenda-vs-list switcher. The selected segment inverts to the dark neutral surface; a CheckSolid icon fades in from the left as the label slides 0.8rem to the right. Renders a <ul> of <button>s (or <a>s) with aria-pressed on the active one.
