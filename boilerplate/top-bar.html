Top Bar

Application-level horizontal chrome anchored to the top of every signed-in screen. Sits above page content on the same deep-navy surface (#00264C) as the OS Menu so the two read as one continuous shell. Hosts global search, contextual pill triggers, a cluster of system-action icon buttons (with optional yellow notification badges), and the user identity at the far right.
