Corner Radii

Seven semantic radius tokens, each aliased onto a grid-unit primitive.

Codified in the Oxygen codebase. Defined in packages/tokens/src/tokens.css as --oxygen-scale-semantic-radius-* — seven tokens, each aliased onto the --oxygen-scale-primitive-gridUnit-* scale. Always reference the semantic name; never hard-code a radius value.
