PatientFileHeader

Identity surface for the patient file. Renders the patient's name, demographics, statuses, contact information and the active recorder selector. Contact info is editable inline; statuses condense into a pill that opens a popover with grouped detail.

One header per patient file. Internally it composes RecorderSelection, StatusesPill, Tag, ContactButton and ContactInformationButton. Loads via usePatient(patientId); renders PatientFileHeaderLoading while pending.
